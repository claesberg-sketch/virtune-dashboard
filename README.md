# Virtune daily AUM & revenue dashboard

Public mirror of a daily-updated dashboard tracking Virtune crypto ETP **assets under management** and **estimated management-fee revenue**.

- **Live site:** https://claesberg-sketch.github.io/virtune-dashboard/
- **Source data:** [virtune.com](https://www.virtune.com/en)
- **Updated:** automatically each weekday morning (Europe/Stockholm)

## Fee estimates only

Figures are **estimated fee-income run-rates** derived from published AUM × management fee. They are **not** Virtune’s accounting revenue. Excludes staking-fee share, creation/redemption spreads, other income, costs, and taxes.
